About Swipe Left

Swipe Left is a personal Islamic resource project dedicated to sharing khutbahs, educational guides, and practical reminders for Muslims.

The name "Swipe Left" reflects the idea of turning away from harmful desires and distractions and instead choosing what pleases Allah.

This site serves as a collection of khutbahs, teaching materials, Islamic reflections, and community resources developed for educational purposes.

Our Mission

To provide accessible Islamic content that encourages knowledge, character, worship, and action.

What You'll Find Here

  • Teaching guides
  • Islamic educational resources
  • Practical applications of Islam in daily life
  • Community projects and initiatives
