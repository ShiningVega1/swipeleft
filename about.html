About Swipe Left

Swipe Left is a personal Islamic project dedicated to sharing khutbahs, educational resources, reminders, and practical guidance.

The phrase "Swipe Left" represents turning away from distractions, temptations, and harmful influences in order to pursue what benefits us in this life and the next.

Mission

To provide beneficial Islamic content that encourages learning, character, worship, and action.

What You'll Find

  • Khutbahs and reminders
  • Teaching guides
  • Islamic resources
  • Community projects
  • Practical applications of Islam
