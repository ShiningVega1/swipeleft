Khutbah: Being Muslim

Target Audience: New Year Reminder / Self-Accountability

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته

My dear brothers and sisters, today is the first khutbah after the new year. Around us, people talk about new beginnings, new goals, and new resolutions. But as Muslims, we stop and reflect, not just on the calendar changing, but on ourselves.

In Islam, time is a blessing and also a responsibility. Every day that passes is written down. Every moment is counted. Allah reminds us how serious time is, and the Prophet ﷺ taught us that we will be questioned about how we spent our lives.

لَا تَزُولُ قَدَمَا عbdٍ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ حَتَّى يُسْأَلُ عَنْ عُمْرِهِ فِيمَا أَفْنَاهُ وَعَنْ عِلْمِهِ فِيمَا فَعَلَ وَعَنْ مَالِهِ مِنْ أَيْنَ اكْتَسَبَهُ وَفِيمَا أَنْفَقَهُ وَعَنْ جِسْمِهِ fِيمَا أَبْلَاهُ

"The feet of the slave of Allah shall not move [on the Day of Judgement] until he is asked about five things: about his life and what he did with it, about his knowledge and what he did with it, about his wealth and how he earned it and where he spent it, and about his body and in what choice he wore it out."

A new year does not erase our mistakes, but it does mean Allah has given us more time. Many people who were alive last year are no longer here. The fact that we are still standing, still breathing, is a mercy from Allah and an opportunity to return to Him.

This is a moment for self-accountability. To ask ourselves honestly: Did I grow closer to Allah last year, or did I grow more distant? Did my salah improve, or did it stay the same? Did I control my tongue, my actions, and my time?

We live in a world full of distractions. Hours disappear on our phones, on social media, on things that will not matter in the grave. But even small changes can make a big difference. One extra prayer on time. A few minutes with the Qur'an. A sincere dua asking Allah to guide us.

My dear brothers and sisters, this year will pass quickly. Before we know it, we will be talking about another year gone. The real question is not how fast time moves—it is who we become as time moves.
