Surah Al-Mulk and Surah Yaseen

Target Audience: Pre-Ramadan Preparation / General Spiritual Boost

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته

My dear brothers and sisters, today is the 25th of Sha'ban, and we are standing at the doorstep of Ramadan. As we prepare our hearts for this blessed month, it is important to reconnect with the Qur'an—not just by reading it, but by building a relationship with it.

Today I want to remind myself and you about two powerful surahs that the Prophet ﷺ encouraged us to keep close to our hearts: Surah Al-Mulk and Surah Yaseen.

Surah Al-Mulk is only thirty verses, yet the Prophet ﷺ told us that it intercedes for a person until they are forgiven. He ﷺ used to recite Surah Al-Mulk every single night before sleeping. This surah reminds us that all control belongs to Allah alone—our lives, our future, and even our final return.

In a world where we stress over grades, popularity, and plans, Surah Al-Mulk reminds us that life and death are tests, and that success is not in this world, but in pleasing Allah.

Surah Yaseen, on the other hand, is known as the heart of the Qur'an. It brings life to the heart just as the heart gives life to the body. Surah Yaseen reminds us of resurrection, accountability, and the mercy of Allah. It softens the heart and brings calm to the soul.

As Ramadan approaches, these two surahs teach us balance. Surah Al-Mulk reminds us of Allah's power, and Surah Yaseen reminds us of Allah's mercy. One wakes us up. The other softens us. Both prepare us for Ramadan. Let us enter the month already holding onto the Book of Allah.

أقيموا الصلاة
