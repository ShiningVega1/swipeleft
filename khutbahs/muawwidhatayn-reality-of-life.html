The Reality of Life and Protection Through the Mu'awwidhāt

Target Audience: General Community / Spiritual Defense

إن الحمد لله، نحمده ونستعينه ونستغفره، ونعوذ بالله من شرور أنفسنا ومن سيِّئات أعمالنا، من يهده الله فلا مُضلَّ له، ومن يُضلل فلا هادي له. وأشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له، وأشهد أن محمدا عبده ورسوله، أما بعد.

One of the greatest realities a believer must remember is that this life is temporary. Allah did not create us for this world alone. This life is a short journey, and every one of us is moving toward the meeting with Allah.

Allah reminds us in the Qur'an that every soul will taste death. No one escapes it. Rich or poor, powerful or weak, every person will return to Allah.

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ explained the nature of this life when he said: "The dunya is a prison for the believer and a paradise for the disbeliever." (Sahih Muslim) For the believer, this world is not the final destination; it is a place of trials, patience, and our preparation for the Hereafter.

Because of this, the Prophet ﷺ gave us a powerful reminder about how we should view this life. He said: "Be in this world as though you were a stranger or a traveler." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

Think of it like driving down a long highway. You wouldn't become deeply attached to the concrete road itself. You know that you will soon leave it behind and reach your final destination. In the same way, the believer understands that this world will pass, and the true life is the life of the Hereafter.

اللَّهُمَّ انْصُرِ الْمُسْتَضْعَفِينَ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ فِي كُلِّ مَكَانٍ.

O Allah, support and aid the oppressed Muslims wherever they are.
