The Gift of Salah (Isra wal-Mi'raj)

Target Audience: Rajab Reminder / Youth Circle

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته

My dear brothers and sisters, we are now in the Islamic month of Rajab, and around this time on the 27th of Rajab - we remember one of the greatest events in our history: Al-Isra wal-Mi'raj, the miraculous night journey of the Prophet ﷺ.

In one night, Allah took the Prophet ﷺ from Makkah to Masjid al-Aqsa, and then raised him through the heavens. He met the prophets, saw signs beyond imagination, and was brought closer to Allah in a way no human ever was. But my dear brothers and sisters, the greatest gift that came from that night was not the journey itself—it was salah.

Salah was not given like other commands. It was not sent down through Jibreel عليه السلام. It was given directly to the Prophet ﷺ above the seven heavens. This alone shows us the value of prayer.

Yet today, many of us struggle with salah. We delay it, rush it, or sometimes miss it completely. Then we wonder why our hearts feel heavy, why stress takes over, and why we feel distant from Allah.

Salah is not a punishment. Salah is a connection. Five times a day, Allah invites us to stand before Him, no matter who we are or what we are going through. School stress, family problems, anxiety - salah is where we place all of that in front of Allah.

The Prophet ﷺ faced hardship, pain, and rejection. Allah did not remove all his struggles, but He gave him salah to survive them. This teaches us something powerful: salah does not remove every problem, but it gives us strength to face them.

As youth, if we want peace in our hearts and barakah in our time, we must protect our prayers. Even fixing one prayer—praying it on time, praying it with focus—can be the beginning of real change. Remember, the first thing we will be asked about on the Day of Judgment is not our grades or our achievements. It will be our salah.

أقيموا الصلاة
